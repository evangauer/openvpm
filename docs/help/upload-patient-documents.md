# Upload patient documents and external lab reports

Keep records received by email, scanned referrals, and laboratory PDFs with the
patient, without retyping the original report.

1. Open **Patients**, choose the patient, then open **Documents**.
2. Under **Add a patient document**, select **External record** or **Lab report**.
3. Choose a PDF, JPG, PNG, or WebP file, then click **Upload document**.
4. Find the attachment in the list. Use **View** or **Download** to retrieve it.
   PDFs may download directly, depending on your browser.

The limit is **4 MB per file**. Compress large scans or split a long record into
smaller PDFs before uploading. Give each file a useful name, such as
`2026-09-13-specialist-referral-part-1.pdf`; the original filename appears in the
patient's list. Upload each part separately.

Use the **Lab reports** filter to find external laboratory attachments. These
files are stored as original reports: uploading does not extract results or
populate structured lab values. Scanned records remain in Documents rather than
the Photos filter, and uploading a record does not change the patient's photo.

If a connection drops or an upload cannot be confirmed, click **Retry upload**.
Keep the same page open: this checks and completes the same operation without
creating another copy. After success, the file appears in the patient's list.

Staff with write access can upload. Viewer accounts can read and download their
practice's documents but cannot add files. Attachments require authorized access;
they are not public links. Self-hosted installations need working primary file
storage, just as patient photo uploads do.
